# Bloom Alexandria – Omnichannel Flower & Gifting Business

Florie is a local flower and gifting business concept in Alexandria, Egypt, combining the personal experience of a physical flower shop with the convenience of an online store.

The business aims to make buying and sending flowers easier, whether customers prefer visiting the shop in person or ordering online for home delivery or surprise gifting across Alexandria.

Beyond selling floral products, Florie focuses on creating meaningful gifting experiences for birthdays, anniversaries, special occasions, and spontaneous gestures. Its proposed digital experience centers on convenient product discovery, occasion-based shopping, personalized gifting, and a consistent brand identity across physical and digital channels.

The goal is to build a recognizable local brand that connects flowers with emotions while making the process of choosing, ordering, and delivering gifts more convenient.

**Project Focus:** Business Positioning, Digital Marketing Strategy, E-commerce, Customer Experience, and Brand Differentiation.

# Work Plan & Marketing Strategy

## Project Workflow

Our work plan follows a structured marketing process designed to build a strong brand presence, understand the target market, attract potential customers, and improve marketing performance over time. The process consists of six main phases, starting with research and brand development, followed by content planning, organic marketing, paid advertising, and continuous optimization.

### 1. Research
We begin by researching the target market, analyzing competitors, understanding customer needs and behavior, and studying market prices. This phase helps us gain valuable insights into the market, identify opportunities, and make informed marketing decisions.

**Goal:** Understand the market, target audience, and competitive landscape.

### 2. Branding
In this phase, we define the brand identity, including its positioning, personality, visual direction, and communication style. The objective is to create a consistent and recognizable brand that communicates its value clearly and stands out from competitors.

**Goal:** Build a distinctive and consistent brand identity.

### 3. Content Plan
Based on the research findings and brand identity, we develop a monthly content plan that outlines content topics, formats, publishing schedules, and communication objectives. The plan is designed to align content with the target audience's interests and the brand's marketing goals.

**Goal:** Increase brand awareness and audience engagement.

### 4. Organic Marketing
We implement the content plan by publishing organic content across relevant social media platforms. This includes Reels, posts, and Stories designed to engage the audience, communicate the brand's value, and build long-term relationships with potential customers.

**Goal:** Build an engaged audience, strengthen trust, and establish a consistent online presence.

### 5. Paid Advertising
We launch targeted advertising campaigns to reach relevant audiences, attract potential customers, and encourage them to take action. Campaigns are developed according to the marketing objectives, target audience, and available budget.

**Goal:** Generate qualified leads, attract customers, and increase orders or conversions.

### 6. Optimization
We continuously monitor and analyze marketing performance using relevant metrics, such as reach, engagement, clicks, conversions, and advertising costs. Based on the results, we identify areas for improvement and optimize content and advertising campaigns to achieve better outcomes.

**Goal:** Improve marketing performance, reduce customer acquisition costs, and maximize return on advertising spend.

---

## Expected Outcome

By following these six phases, we aim to establish a strong brand foundation, create meaningful connections with the target audience, increase brand awareness, generate customer interest, and continuously improve marketing results through data-driven decisions.
